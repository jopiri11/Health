Step‑by‑step admission

  • 1 Check symptoms first – Use our symptom checker or call our triage nurse.
  • 2 Get a consultation – Visit OPD or emergency. Bring ID & insurance card.
  • 3 Admission desk – Complete paperwork, choose room category, receive a patient ID.
  • 4 Pre‑admission tests – Labs, imaging (if required) before moving to your room.
  • 5 Treatment & recovery – Our team ensures round‑the‑clock care.
